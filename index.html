Hi there

I'm Sanket, a Business Intelligence Engineer at Amazon in Seattle, five years in. At Amazon, BIE is an analytics and data engineering role: I design and build the pipelines, dimensional models and self-serve reporting that business, finance, operations and external partners run on.

I tend to end up on the systems nobody owns. Two of my main projects were inherited under pressure, both were on fire when I got them, and both are still running. My focus is the full stack from ingestion and transformation through modeling to the BI layer, with an emphasis on reliability, automation and catching a bad number before anyone downstream sees it.
